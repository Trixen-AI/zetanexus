ZetaNexus: Private ZEC in. Onchain settlement out.

Accept shielded ZEC payments. Settle merchant orders on Robinhood Chain. Your customer keeps the privacy of the Zcash shielded pool, and you keep a settlement hash you can hand to an auditor.

How it works

  1. Create a quote: a live ZEC price, a single-use shielded address and a 15 minute expiry.
  2. Receive ZEC: sender, amount and memo stay encrypted on the Zcash blockchain.
  3. Verify settlement: the payout lands on Robinhood Chain as a public, verifiable transaction.
